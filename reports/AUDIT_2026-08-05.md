## 2. FAKE PROFIT RISK

Ranked by how much each inflates the numbers.

### 🔴 1. The headline backtest does not charge the fee it actually pays

`BacktestEngine.__init__(use_fee_schedule=False)` is the default and **no caller in the repo ever sets it `True`**. `portfolio/system.py:71` constructs the engine with `cost_per_turnover=COST` only. So `backtest/fees.py` — the one module that models the flat **EGP 4.95 per order** — never touches a single headline number.

Every published figure (CAGR 28.4%, Sharpe 1.59–1.92, MaxDD −31% to −42%) is computed at a flat 0.55% round trip, a **percentage-only** model.

Measured against reality on 2026-08-04–05:
- 1,000 EGP book, 8 names → **8.2% round trip** (15× the assumption)
- 10,000 EGP book, 12 names → **1.5% round trip** (2.7× the assumption)

Worse, the two ends cannot be reconciled: at small size the unmodelled flat fee dominates; at the size where flat fees vanish, `analysis/capacity.py` puts the ceiling at ~12.5M EGP and `analysis/real_returns.py` already shows real CAGR ≈ 0 there. **There is no capital level at which the headline numbers are achievable.**

### 🔴 2. Survivorship + selection bias in the universe

`config.EGX_SEED_TICKERS` — 47 names, 20 added 2026-07-28 explicitly ranked by *2026* liquidity — is applied to a 2015–2026 backtest. Companies that delisted, collapsed or became illiquid over that window are structurally absent. `data/universe_discovery.py` states the problem plainly and does not fix it.

Magnitude is unmeasured, which is itself the problem. On an emerging market over 11 years this is not a rounding error.

### 🟡 3. Modelled slippage is 27× below measured slippage

`config.SLIPPAGE_PCT = 0.0010`. `ops/execution_quality.py` on your first 8 real fills reports **weighted mean shortfall +2.745%**, 88% unfavourable. Part of that is a stale signal price (2-session-old reference), so it overstates true slippage — but the modelled figure has now been contradicted by every fill on record and nothing downstream has been updated.

### 🟡 4. Live rebalance cadence is not the backtested cadence

Backtests rebalance every 21 sessions (`BacktestEngine(rebalance_days=21)`). The live path re-evaluates **every day** (`ops/portfolio_tracker.reconcile`). Acting on daily signals would raise turnover above what was costed. The 2.73pp effective threshold makes frequent trading unlikely in practice — but the difference is untested, and it can only push real costs *up*.

### 🟡 5. In-sample tuning presented alongside forward evidence

The ensemble design, `bear_scale`, `NAME_CAP_*`, `MAX_FEE_SHARE_OF_TRADE` and `REBALANCE_THRESHOLD` were all chosen on the same single EGX history the performance is quoted from. `analysis/deflated_sharpe.py` exists and correctly reports failure at N=1 — but the DSR trial count does not reflect the true number of design decisions taken against this data.

### ℹ️ Correctly handled — no risk

- **Sentiment/TFT are not silently contributing.** 0 of 64,080 rows carry sentiment; neither layer is imported by `ops/daily_run.py`. They inflate the project's apparent sophistication but not its returns.
- **Causal leakage is contained.** The pooled-θ function still exists but the entire causal layer is off the live path, and `LIVE_MIN_VAR_ONLY=True` raises if anyone tries to route selection into a trade list.
- **HMM log-likelihood decrease is detected, not ignored** (`models/hmm.py:200` logs a warning). The ensemble in `models/regime_ensemble.py` is the structural mitigation.
- **Forward record is not blended into backtest statistics** anywhere.

## 3. Top 5 most dangerous gaps

**1. The cost model that produces every headline number is not the cost model you pay** *(Section 9)*
`backtest/fees.py` is dead code with respect to results. This is the single largest distortion, it is systematic, and it flatters exactly the small-book regime you actually trade. Until `use_fee_schedule=True` is the default, no quoted Sharpe or CAGR is a claim about your account.

**2. Survivorship-biased universe over an 11-year emerging-market backtest** *(Section 4/10b)*
Unquantified and unbounded. Every historical statistic inherits it. This cannot be fixed by better modelling — it needs delisted-name price history the project does not have.

**3. Zero bear-regime days in the live record** *(Sections 12, 25)*
The system's *only* claimed edge is BEAR→cash. It has never been observed working forward. 19 days, +4.81%, all in BULL. The go-live gate correctly refuses, but the temptation to read +4.81% as validation is the live danger.

**4. No transaction-cost sensitivity analysis** *(Section 17)*
Given gap #1, the obvious question — does the edge survive 2× or 3× assumed costs — has never been asked. With the real schedule already 2.7–15× the assumption depending on size, this is not hypothetical.

**5. Risk is computed but never binds** *(Section 14)*
`risk/` produces numbers for reports. The only control that can actually stop a trade is the kill switch, and it operates on the whole book. Position-size limits, correlation limits and ADV participation are advisory strings. A concentrated or illiquid order will be printed with a warning and nothing more.

## 4. Overall score

### **40–60%**

Engineering discipline is genuinely high for a solo project — hard-failing data gates, a wired kill switch, walk-forward regime refits, deflated-Sharpe testing, honest recording of null results, and 401 passing tests. The research layer is more intellectually honest than most commercial systems: it has repeatedly proven its own hypotheses false and documented that rather than hiding it.

But the checklist is being scored on whether the *numbers can be trusted*, and two 🔴s are structural rather than incremental: **the backtest does not charge the fee schedule the project itself measured**, and **the universe is survivorship-biased across the entire backtest window**. Either alone makes historical performance non-comparable to achievable performance; together they mean the quoted Sharpe is not an estimate of anything realisable.

### Does a single 🔴 override?

**Yes — Section 9 overrides.** Had the cost wiring been correct, the discipline elsewhere would justify 60–75%. It is not a modelling subtlety: it is a one-line default that disconnects the measured fee schedule from every result, and the error runs entirely in the flattering direction.

The one saving grace is that the system already refuses to act on its own numbers. `GO_LIVE_MIN_PAPER_DAYS=189`, `GO_LIVE_MIN_BEAR_DAYS=20`, `LIVE_MIN_VAR_ONLY=True` and the kill switch mean the backtest's optimism is not currently being converted into risk. **The scoring failure is one of evidence, not of live exposure.**
